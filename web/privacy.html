Your location. The app reads your location only while it is open, to show your distance. When you log, your exact position is sent once to work out your distance and region, then thrown away. We store the distance, the state or country, and the time, never your coordinates.

Your home pin. You choose a home spot during setup. It is stored so the game can tell when you are home, and only you can see it.

What friends see. Your name, username, distances, streak, points and the spots you have claimed. Never your location or home. Logs from home are not shown to friends.

Your account. We keep your email or Apple/Google sign-in id, your name and username, and your device's push token. You can delete your account in the app at any time, which removes all of it.

Store data. McDonald's locations come from the open AllThePlaces dataset. We have no connection with McDonald's.

We do not sell your data or use it for ads.
